Changing the site's style
--------------------------
No Jekyll theme; _layouts/default.html is hand-written. Four interchangeable stylesheets
live under css/, but only one is active at a time (whichever the <link> in
_layouts/default.html points at):

  css/plain.css      - empty; classic unstyled HTML look
  css/plain2.css     - plain.css + just a centered max-width column (current default)
  css/style.css      - the old Cayman theme look
  css/principia.css  - matches principia-softwarica.org and aryx.github.io/xix

To switch, edit the stylesheet <link> in _layouts/default.html, e.g.:

  <link rel="stylesheet" href="{{ '/css/principia.css' | relative_url }}">

See CLAUDE.md for the fuller technical writeup (why there's no theme, etc).
