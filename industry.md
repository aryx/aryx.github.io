# Industry

Full profile: [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yoann-padioleau-6379268/)

- **r2c / Semgrep** (2019–2025) — joined as sgrep's original author (employee #13); the team
  rebuilt it into [Semgrep](https://github.com/semgrep/semgrep) (r2c later renamed itself Semgrep)
  in 2020. Over that time the company grew from ~20 to 150+ employees and from Series B to D
  funding. See Isaac Evans' (r2c's CEO) post
  [Semgrep: stop grepping code](https://semgrep.dev/blog/2020/semgrep-stop-grepping-code/) for how
  he credits sgrep and Coccinelle, my earlier tools, as Semgrep's origin.
- **Facebook** (Oct 2009 – Oct 2014) — first static analysis hire; introduced OCaml internally and
  built [pfff](https://github.com/facebookarchive/pfff), which grew into
  [codemap](https://github.com/aryx/codemap), [codegraph](https://github.com/aryx/codegraph), and
  sgrep.
- **Pattern Insight** (2007) — a few months, during my postdoc, at this small startup doing code
  search and static analysis tools, co-founded by Yuanyuan Zhou (my [ICSE 2009](papers.md)
  co-author) and Spiros Xanthos; later acquired by VMware. Both went on to found several more
  companies — Zhou co-founded Whova; Xanthos co-founded Omnition (acquired by Splunk) and is now
  CEO of Resolve AI.
